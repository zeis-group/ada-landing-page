For term life insurance, premiums increase annually based on age. Universal life recommended premiums consist of the costs of insurance (which may vary based on the member’s age and coverage amount), the amount a member places into the Policy Value Account and service charge. Premium deposits may fluctuate or remain level. Premium credit is not guaranteed but reevaluated periodically.

For level term life insurance, premiums are fixed based on the member’s issue age and coverage amount.

For disability income protection insurance, premiums increase every five years based on age. For office overhead expense disability insurance, premiums increase every 10 years based on age to age 60, and every 5 years thereafter to age 75. Premium credit is not guaranteed but reevaluated periodically.

Coverage for dental student members is issued regardless of condition if under age 60. Coverage automatically renews each academic year. Individuals may convert coverage to the plans for practicing dentists after graduation by paying member premiums and maintaining continuous ADA membership.
